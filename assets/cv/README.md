# Youngkwon Ko - CV

The editable source is `youngkwon-ko-cv.tex`. Compile twice with XeLaTeX:

```sh
xelatex youngkwon-ko-cv.tex
xelatex youngkwon-ko-cv.tex
```

The design matches the portfolio: warm paper (#F8F7F3), charcoal text, muted green, thin rules, serif headings and sans-serif text. TeX Gyre fonts are preferred; Latin Modern Sans and URW P052 are used when needed, with a Latin Modern Roman fallback.

The two-page English CV preserves the user-supplied CV content and its five sections; it omits added research interests, ongoing projects and software. Creai+it ends in December 2025. Navy service is ongoing with an expected completion in August 2027. Only the two publications already cited in the supplied CV are included. The CCTV manuscript is omitted because its publication status and venue have not been established.

About ends with direct PDF and LaTeX downloads. There is no preview or separate CV page. Update the file URLs and date in `manifest.js` after editing and compiling the source.

LaTeX auxiliary files and intermediate PNGs are not shipped with the site.
